# Design QA — citizen gateway v2

**Checked:** 22 August 2026  
**Scope:** local prototype at `http://127.0.0.1:4173/` using synthetic data and mocked integrations.

## What was exercised

- Fresh citizen entry without exposing case data.
- Challan, vehicle, and driving-licence lookup modes.
- Server-issued arithmetic challenge, invalid/valid handling, and refresh path.
- Synthetic case lookup using the documented vehicle number.
- Amount hierarchy and pay-or-contest decision area.
- Mock payment confirmation, provider success, ledger posting, receipt, timeline, and audit event.
- Wrong-vehicle contest submission, reviewer quash decision, and citizen status update.
- English/Hindi/Telugu gateway switching, high-contrast mode, skip link, and text controls.
- Responsive entry page at 390 x 844 without horizontal overflow.

## Result

The v2 entry follows the recognisable official sequence—identifier, human verification, case result, then resolution—without cloning government identity. The amount is now an independent cream-and-gold decision card rather than competing with the offence banner. Citizen services converge on one protected lookup, and potentially confusing reviewer access is explicitly marked as a demo.

The payment experience is deliberately safe: it does not collect UPI IDs, account numbers, card details, passwords, or OTPs. Its backend transition is real inside the local prototype, with idempotency, separate provider/ledger states, a receipt, timeline entry, and audit event.

## Known boundaries before a public pilot

- Only the gateway has an English/Hindi/Telugu pilot; complete case, grievance, payment, receipt, and reviewer localization is not implemented.
- Hindi and Telugu terminology needs review by native public-service translators.
- The arithmetic check demonstrates an accessible server challenge; it is not production-grade bot or abuse protection.
- Automated axe testing and manual NVDA/Firefox testing are not complete, so WCAG 2.1 AA conformance is not claimed.
- The official journey after CAPTCHA was not exercised; no official CAPTCHA was solved or bypassed.
- Pending-payment reconciliation, production authentication, real government/payment adapters, rate limits, PostgreSQL, and deployment are deferred.
