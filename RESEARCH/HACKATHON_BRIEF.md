# Build What Moves India — working brief

## Intent

The competition asks builders to rethink public-service websites. The strongest interpretation is not “make a prettier government homepage”; it is to identify a consequential public journey, remove its friction, and prove the improved service with a working build.

## Relevant competition requirements

- Deadline: 27 August 2026.
- Current published finale: 12 September 2026.
- Ten finalists.
- Up to four people per team; submitter at least 18.
- Codex is required in the build process.
- Every demonstrated feature must work.
- Public demo without access restrictions.
- Demo video no longer than three minutes, plus a write-up; source repository optional.
- Synthetic/mock data is appropriate where live government data or sandboxes are unavailable.
- No undocumented API use, official-logo misuse, or misleading official affiliation.
- Open-source and external tooling must be disclosed.
- Builders retain their work.

## What the judges should understand in 20 seconds

> Wrong and unclear traffic challans are not just a form-design problem. Challan Nyay connects citizen guidance, evidence, jurisdiction routing, human review, reasoned orders, and status into one working resolution loop.

## Selection strategy

### Table stakes

- visibly better mobile experience;
- fast and reliable live demo;
- accessible, multilingual-ready, plain-language UI;
- polished visual system;
- clear source attribution and truthful mock labels.

### Differentiators

- dual citizen and authority experiences;
- Rule 167 deadlines expressed as an operational state machine;
- evidence-aware guided contest, not a generic grievance text box;
- jurisdiction adapter architecture that explains nationwide scale honestly;
- end-to-end case movement, real audit events, and reasoned decisions;
- low-bandwidth resilience and interrupted-flow recovery;
- AI used for administrative assistance, never judgment.

### Proof over pitch

The demo should spend little time on a marketing homepage. Most time should show the case moving from citizen to reviewer and back. Any claim in the write-up should point to a demo-visible interaction, acceptance test, or clearly marked plan.

## Submission story

1. **Scale:** official data shows tens of millions of challans and sharply rising complaints.
2. **Human problem:** a wrong challan creates uncertainty across portals, evidence, money, deadlines, and court handoffs.
3. **Policy moment:** the January 2026 Rule 167 changes create a digital contest and reasoned-resolution opportunity.
4. **Product:** one guided, accessible, nationwide-by-design case journey.
5. **Operational proof:** authority queue, SLA, evidence packet, reason, order, timeline.
6. **Trust:** independent prototype, synthetic data, no hidden integrations, safe AI boundary.
