# Two-minute submission demo storyboard

Target runtime: 1:50–1:58. Every shot uses the live product and synthetic data.

## 0:00–0:12 — problem and promise

Visual: landing screen and vehicle-first lookup.

> A wrong or unclear challan becomes an evidence, deadline, payment and authority-routing problem. Challan Nyay makes the complete path understandable and accountable.

## 0:12–0:30 — two entry modes

Visual: show the prefilled vehicle lookup briefly, then the optional account entry and dashboard.

Show:

- guest lookup is vehicle first;
- synthetic OTP is visible and sends no SMS;
- one account can manage multiple authorized vehicles;
- no real identity or government system is contacted.

## 0:30–0:55 — understand the notice

Visual: challan detail.

Show:

- metadata and contest date;
- original synthetic enforcement frame;
- derived number-plate crop;
- fixed-camera source and OpenStreetMap location;
- plain-language mismatch explanation;
- amount separated from pay/contest choice.

## 0:55–1:18 — dispute with evidence

Visual: choose the supported wrong-vehicle ground and submit.

Show:

- selected challan summary;
- evidence packet and citizen statement;
- synthetic declaration;
- durable receipt, current owner and timeline.

## 1:18–1:42 — human resolution

Visual: reviewer demo.

Show:

- the same case and evidence comparison;
- human quash decision with structured reason;
- order reference and append-only audit event;
- citizen timeline updated with the outcome.

## 1:42–1:55 — architecture and close

Visual: return to dashboard/detail; overlay a compact architecture caption if needed.

> One citizen experience, state-configured rules, and replaceable adapters for identity, vehicle registries, enforcement, payments and maps. This prototype uses only synthetic data and mocked authority connectors, but the resolution loop really works.

## Recording rules

- keep the run under two minutes;
- use the visible demo values and rehearse twice from a clean session;
- do not solve the human check off camera or hide latency with speed ramps;
- use readable captions and cursor/focus;
- show no personal tabs, secrets, real identifiers or official branding;
- claims in the video, live site and 250-word description must match;
- keep a local recording as fallback.
