# Competition and project rules

This file converts the official Build What Moves India brief and FAQ into operational constraints. Re-check the live pages before submission because organizer details can change.

## Competition snapshot checked on 27 August 2026

- Submission deadline: 28 August 2026 at 8:00 PM IST; the form has no grace period.
- Current published finale date: 12 September 2026.
- Stage 1 shortlists 250 builds; Stage 2 selects ten finalists.
- Teams may contain up to two people; both teammates must register and cross-reference the other's registered email. Participants must be at least 18.
- Registration is an expression of interest, not selection or confirmation.
- OpenAI Codex must be used in the build process.
- Every feature shown in the demo must work.
- The demo must be publicly accessible without special access restrictions.
- Required submission materials are a live public browser link, a video no longer than two minutes, and a project summary under 250 words. Minute one should show the citizen journey; minute two should explain the build and product choices.
- Reviewers test the citizen experience, not an admin panel. Mock consumer credentials are required only if the citizen experience needs login.
- Builders retain ownership of their work.

## Must do

- Solve a concrete public-service problem and demonstrate a meaningful improvement.
- Make the citizen-facing and operational outcomes visible in the live demo.
- Use synthetic or mock data and clearly label all mocked connections.
- Keep the live demo unrestricted and test it in a signed-out/private browser before submission.
- Disclose open-source libraries, external tools, AI systems, fonts, icons, and assets.
- Make the build reproducible and keep a fallback local recording.
- Ensure the video, live site, and written claims describe the same working scope.
- Include the independent-prototype disclaimer.

## Must not do

- Do not use a live government system unless an official sandbox and documented authorization are provided.
- Do not call undocumented APIs, scrape protected portals, automate CAPTCHA, or bypass controls.
- Do not use real citizen data, real challans, real documents, real OTPs, or real payments.
- Do not imply endorsement or use official logos, seals, emblems, or copied identity systems.
- Do not show non-working interactions in the video or describe mock behavior as integrated.
- Do not promise selection, adoption, legal accuracy, or production readiness.
- Do not submit copyrighted assets or code without a compatible licence.

## Submission quality bar

The entry is ready only when:

1. The public URL works without login or an access request.
2. The two-minute citizen-demo path succeeds twice from a clean session.
3. All seeded case identifiers and reviewer credentials are visible in the demo itself.
4. Every AI output is reviewable, attributable to input, and safe to correct.
5. Every consequential case transition creates a real audit event.
6. Mobile, keyboard, screen-reader landmarks, slow-network, and narrow-screen paths pass.
7. No real data or secrets appear in the repository, UI, logs, video, or browser history.
8. The write-up states what is implemented, mocked, and future work.
