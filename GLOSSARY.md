# Glossary

- **Challan:** a notice alleging a traffic offence and specifying a penalty or further process.
- **Contest:** the Rule 167 digital submission of grounds and documentary evidence against a challan.
- **Grievance:** a broader portal complaint or service issue; it is not always equivalent to a statutory contest.
- **Jurisdiction:** the state, UT, police, transport, or court authority responsible for a case.
- **Authority order:** the uploaded, reasoned decision after review.
- **Quashed:** the challan ceases to have effect following the applicable process.
- **Rejected:** the contest was not accepted; the citizen receives reasons and next-step options.
- **SLA clock:** the time measure used by the service to show and monitor the applicable resolution window.
- **Evidence packet:** normalized metadata and citizen-provided files assembled for review.
- **Adapter:** a replaceable boundary that represents an external government, court, identity, payment, or notification connection.
- **Mock adapter:** an in-project simulation used for the hackathon; it performs no external transaction.
- **Synthetic data:** fictional people, vehicles, challans, documents, and transactions created only for demonstration and testing.
- **Reason code:** a structured review outcome paired with a plain-language explanation.
- **Audit event:** an append-only record of a consequential action, actor, time, and correlation identifier.
- **Rule 167:** the Central Motor Vehicles Rules procedure described in G.S.R. 48(E), dated 20 January 2026, for issuance, contest, payment, and related time windows.
